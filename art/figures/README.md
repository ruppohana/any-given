# Figures

Football stick figures **drawn by Jason**, 2026-09-11 ("I drew theses"). Ours to ship:
no license, no attribution. `node tools/figures.mjs` cuts them into
`public/art/fig-*.png` — alpha masks in the shape of each card's tiles, every figure on
a card drawn at one helmet size. Re-run it after changing a sheet or a crop box.

| Sheet | What the cards use from it |
|---|---|
| `jason-sheet-2026-09-11.png` — his first six figures | the passer, the ball carrier and the kicker at the tee (run or pass; go for it or kick) |
| `jason-library-2026-09-11.png` — about fifty labeled players, referee signals and field objects | tackle, fumble, punt, punt return, dropped pass, celebration, field-goal attempt, goal posts, touchdown signal, ball on the tee |
| `jason-picks-2026-09-11.png` — a picked set | Run 2, the ball carrier with speed lines (ten yards; break one) |
| `jason-chain-crew-2026-09-11.png` — the chain crew | the linesman with the "1" pole (move the chains) |
| `jason-down-markers-2026-09-11.png` — the sticks and down boxes 1-4 | the box showing 4 ("They are stopped") |
| `jason-first-down-signal-2026-09-11.png` — the referee pointing | the front-facing one (convert on 3rd; three and out) |

Only sheets something uses are kept here (Jason: "Delete anything we don't use
anymore"). The markers-and-chain sheet he also sent was removed on that basis; it is in
git history.

**Never in this folder:** the two stock silhouettes (a passer and a runner) Jason sent
the same day as samples. They were never bought and must not be committed or shipped.
