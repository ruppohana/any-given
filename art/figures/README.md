# Figures

`jason-sheet-2026-09-11.png` — six football stick figures **drawn by Jason**, 2026-09-11
("I drew theses"). Ours to ship: no license, no attribution.

The call cards use the left column ("I like the run/pass/kick on the left"): the passer
(row 1), the ball carrier (row 2) and the kicker at the tee (row 3). The right column —
the thrower with the ball in flight, a second ball carrier and the punter — is unused so
far; the punter is earmarked for *How does this drive end?* and *Three and out?*.

Later the same day Jason sent a whole library in the same style ("Look these over"),
saved here as sent:

- `jason-library-2026-09-11.png` — about fifty labeled figures: throw, run, catch, handoff,
  snap, kick, tackle, sack, fumble, fumble recovery, interception, incomplete pass, punt,
  punt return, dropped pass, touchdown, field-goal attempt, field goal good, extra point,
  two-point conversion, celebration, spike, pylon, safety; ten referee signals; and the
  down marker, yard markers, chain set, football, football on tee, helmet, goal posts,
  scoreboard, play clock and huddle.
- `jason-down-markers-2026-09-11.png` — the chain-gang markers, down boxes 1-4 and the chain.
- `jason-markers-chain-2026-09-11.png` — the two sticks, the down box and the chain.
- `jason-chain-crew-2026-09-11.png` — the chain crew holding them.
- `jason-picks-2026-09-11.png` — a picked set: throws, runs with speed lines, the diving
  fumble and recovery, holder and kicker, the chain crew, goal posts, the touchdown signal.

`node tools/figures.mjs` cuts them into `public/art/fig-*.png` (alpha masks, both figures
on a card at one scale). Re-run it after changing the sheet or the crop boxes.

**Not in this folder, deliberately:** the two stock silhouettes (a passer and a runner)
Jason sent earlier the same day as samples. They were never bought and must not be
committed or shipped.
