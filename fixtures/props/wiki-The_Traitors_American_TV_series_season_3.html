Season 4

The third season of the American television series The Traitors was announced on February 7, 2024.[1] The season premiered on January 9, 2025.[2] The season concluded on March 6, 2025 where Dolores Catania, Dylan Efron, Gabby Windey and Lord Ivar Mountbatten won as Faithfuls, while Britney Haynes placed as a runner-up, as a Traitor.[3]

Production

Similar to the previous seasons, primary filming takes place in Ardross Castle in the Scottish Highlands.[4]

Contestants

From left to right: Dorinda Medley, Bob the Drag Queen, Nikki Garcia, Bob Harper, Derrick Levasseur, Rob Mariano, Chrishell Stause, Sam Asghari, Tom Sandoval, Dolores Catania, Dylan Efron, and Gabby Windey

Similar to the second season, this season's cast was composed entirely of reality show participants and public figures. The 21 original players were announced on June 5, 2024,[5] with two contestants (Wes and Derrick) entering the game in Episode 2 without a prior public announcement.

Notes
  1. ↑ No murder took place; having become the Seer, Britney selected Gabby to meet with her and reveal her identity.
  2. ↑ After the Round Table in Episode 3, the Traitors were tasked to meet in plain sight to make a shortlist of three players to go inside a coffin where the Traitors would nail one of them shut, sealing their fate as that round’s murder. The Traitors decided to murder Jeremy.
  3. ↑ Following Carolyn's banishment, Danielle was offered the chance to meet a Faithful face-to-face and offer them an ultimatum: become a Traitor or be Murdered. She chose Britney, who accepted the offer.
  4. 1 2 3 The first round of voting resulted in a tie. Per the rules, a second vote was held where the players involved in the tie would not vote and the remaining players could only vote for those who tied.
  5. ↑ Danielle's banishment was revealed in Episode 11 after the tied vote ended on a cliffhanger in Episode 10.
  6. ↑ At the beginning of the game, Alan offered the players a chance for Rob to enter the game by shaking his hand. If someone were to accept this offer, they would have to exile another player of their choice. Nobody took the offer, and thus Rob was exiled.
  7. ↑ Derrick and Wes, along with the returning Rob, were introduced to the game in Episode 2. Upon their introduction, they could not be voted for or murdered that night. They were also not allowed to vote at the Round Table that night.

11 "The Day of Reckoning is Upon Us" $50,000 $37,500 $204,300 —N/a

The final mission was done in two parts. In the first part the players had to solve riddles and locate the bags of money within 30 minutes. In the second part, the players had to volunteer to hang beneath a helicopter with bags of gold, which they had to drop into a burning ring of fire. The players done this in two pairs and one player would do it solo. Each bag was worth $2,500 which would be doubled if it landed in the ring of fire or stay the same if it didn't. There was no time limit for the second part.
