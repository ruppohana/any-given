Put Any Given on your home screen

Then it opens like an app: full screen, no browser bars, one tap away on game day.

iPhone Android

One thing first. Do these steps on the main Any Given page, not on this help page. If you add this help page, the icon will open this help page. Read the steps, then go:
Open Any Given

iPhone

Use the Safari app. It takes about a minute.

  1. 1Open Safari.

    Find the Safari app on your Home Screen and tap it. It is usually in the row at the very bottom of the screen.

    You will see: Safari opens.

  2. 2Go to anygiven.app.

    Type anygiven.app in the address bar. That is the long bar that shows the web address. Or tap the blue Open Any Given button near the top of this page.

    You will see: the Any Given page.

  3. 3Tap the three lines.

    Look at the very bottom of the screen. Right next to anygiven.app, on its left side, are three short lines stacked up (☰). Tap them.

    You will see: a menu pops up.

    Do you see three dots (···) or a square with an arrow pointing up instead? Your iPhone has an older Safari. Tap the dots, or tap the square and skip to step 5.

  4. 4Tap Share.

    In the menu, find the word Share and tap it. It is the first one.

    You will see: a list of choices slides up from the bottom of the screen.

  5. 5Tap View More, then Add to Home Screen.

    A list slides up. In the row of round buttons, tap View More (the one on the far right). More choices open underneath. Tap Add to Home Screen. It is the last one.

    You will see: a card called Add to Home Screen, with the Any Given icon and the name Any Given.

    No View More button? Slide the list up with your finger instead. Still not there? Go to the very bottom and tap Edit Actions, then Add to Home Screen.

  6. 6Check that Open as Web App is on.

    Find the switch next to Open as Web App. It is usually already green, which means on. If it is gray, tap it. Leave the name as Any Given.

    You will see: the switch is green.

  7. 7Tap Add.

    Tap Add, at the top right of the card.

    You will see: the card goes away, and the Any Given icon is on your Home Screen.

Can't find it on iPhone?

Use the Safari app. If Any Given opened inside another app, like Instagram, Facebook or a text message, or in a different browser like Chrome, open anygiven.app in Safari. Then start again at step 1.

Android

Use the Chrome app. It takes about a minute.

  1. 1Open Chrome.

    Find the Chrome app on your home screen and tap it. It is often in the row at the bottom of the screen.

    You will see: Chrome opens.

  2. 2Go to anygiven.app.

    Type anygiven.app in the address bar at the top of the screen. Or tap the blue Open Any Given button near the top of this page.

    You will see: the Any Given page.

  3. 3Tap the three dots.

    Look at the top right, just to the right of the address bar. Tap the three dots stacked on top of each other (⋮).

    You will see: a menu drops down.

    Is your address bar at the bottom of the screen? Then the three dots are at the bottom right.

  4. 4Tap Install and create shortcut.

    In the menu, find Install and create shortcut and tap it.

    You will see: a small box pops up with an Install button.

  5. 5Tap Install.

    In the box, tap Install. If your phone asks anything else, follow what it says on the screen.

    You will see: Any Given is added to your home screen.

Can't find it on Android?

Use the Chrome app. If Any Given opened inside another app, like Instagram or a text message, open anygiven.app in Chrome. Still no Install and create shortcut? Update Chrome in the Play Store, then try again.

You did it

Any Given

Look on your home screen for a stadium with little red flags on top, and the name Any Given under it.

Tap it any time. Any Given opens full screen, just like an app.

Don't see it? Swipe left through your home screen pages. It may be on the last one.
