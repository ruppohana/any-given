Privacy

Effective September 10, 2026

Any Given is a free football game. Nothing in it can be bought, and nothing you win can be cashed out. This page says what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. We don't use passwords.
  • Your first and last name, and a handle you choose. Your handle is what other players see on standings and boards; your full name is not shown to them.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older. Any Given is for adults only, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
  • What you do in the game: your picks, your calls, your Marbles, the groups you create or join, and the display name you choose, so other people in your group can see the standings.
  • A random device identifier stored on your phone, so the app recognizes it between visits.

What we use it for

  • Signing you in, and making sure nobody else can make picks as you.
  • Showing standings to the people in your groups.
  • Keeping the game working and fixing it when it breaks.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts or payment details.

Who handles it for us

The app runs on Cloudflare, which stores the game's data. Sign-in codes are emailed through Resend. Each handles your information only to provide that service.

Your choices

You can delete your account yourself, any time, in the app: ⋮ menu → Your account → Delete account. That removes your account, your picks, your marbles history and your group memberships; a group you run passes to its longest-standing member. You can also ask us to show you or delete the information tied to your email.

Do Not Track

We don't track you across other sites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
